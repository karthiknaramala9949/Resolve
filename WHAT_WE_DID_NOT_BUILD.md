# What We Did NOT Build — And Why

> An architectural record of deliberate omissions, ethical boundaries, and design trade-offs in RESOLVE-ONE.

In consumer software, **what you choose NOT to build defines your integrity more than what you ship**. Throughout the development of RESOLVE-ONE, multiple features commonly found in venture-backed consumer apps and legal-tech prototypes were deliberately rejected. 

Here is what we omitted and the precise engineering, statutory, and ethical reasons behind each decision.

---

## 1. No Cloud User Authentication (No Email/Password or SMS OTP Login)

### What was omitted:
A mandatory user login screen requiring email addresses, phone numbers, Google OAuth, or SMS OTP verification before accessing the application.

### Why:
1. **Zero Barrier to Relief**: A consumer whose ₹35,000 laptop was delivered with a shattered screen does not want to remember a password, verify an email link, or wait for an SMS OTP that fails on low-signal mobile networks.
2. **Elimination of Data Honeypots**: Storing dispute records, bank transaction details, and purchase invoices in a centralized cloud database makes the server a prime target for credential stuffing, data breaches, and corporate subpoenas.
3. **Local-First Empowerment**: By storing all cases, documents, and evidence blobs directly in the browser's IndexedDB (`resolve-one-v2`) behind an abstract repository pattern, the user retains complete ownership of their data on their own device.
4. **Zero Account Lockout Risk**: Users retain instant access to their case records offline on flights, trains, or during network blackouts. For users demanding maximum privacy, local WebCrypto AES-256-GCM passphrase encryption protects evidence on shared family computers without transmitting any data over the internet.

---

## 2. No Fake "AI" or Cloud LLM Wrappers

### What was omitted:
Promising an "AI Lawyer", "AI Dispute Negotiator", or routing user narratives through cloud LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, or Gemini) to summarize or evaluate complaints.

### Why:
1. **Statutory Integrity & CPA 2019**: Consumer disputes in India are governed by the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, statutory e-commerce rules, and Reserve Bank of India (RBI) ombudsman frameworks. Hallucinations, invented precedents, or vague legal summaries generated by LLMs could actively harm a consumer's statutory claim before a District Consumer Commission.
2. **Privacy of Financial & Personal Narratives**: Sending unredacted consumer complaints (which frequently include order IDs, bank names, UPI transaction references, and phone numbers) to third-party AI APIs introduces serious privacy and compliance risks.
3. **Latency and Offline Availability**: Large language models require steady broadband connectivity and introduce 3 to 10 seconds of streaming latency. Our deterministic, client-side regex engine (`intakeParser.ts`) extracts companies, disputed amounts, order numbers, and complaint categories in **under 4 milliseconds entirely offline**.
4. **Honest Labeling**: We explicitly label all suggested chips:  
   *"Suggested from your words (local rule-based parsing, not AI)"*.  
   We never mislead a stressed user into believing an artificial intelligence is reviewing their case.

---

## 3. No Automated Scraping or Headless Submission to Government & Corporate Portals

### What was omitted:
A "1-Tap Auto-File" bot that logs into government portals (e-Jagriti, National Consumer Helpline 1915, RBI CMS) or e-commerce ticket forms (Flipkart, Amazon) using customer credentials to file complaints automatically.

### Why:
1. **Statutory Identity Verification**: Official Indian dispute avenues (such as e-Jagriti and NCH) legally require consumer authentication via Aadhaar OTP, mobile OTP, or DigiLocker. Automating these systems would require intercepting OTPs, defeating CAPTCHAs, or creating unauthorized bot sessions—violating portal terms of service and cyber law (Information Technology Act, 2000).
2. **Zero Credential Theft**: Asking users for their Amazon, Flipkart, or banking passwords would violate our core honesty boundary.
3. **The Guided Handoff Pattern**: Rather than unsafe automation, RESOLVE-ONE implements the **Guided Handoff**:
   - The user compiles an ordered, cryptographic **Proof Pack PDF** with SHA-256 evidence hashes.
   - The user copies a pre-formatted formal legal notice citing CPA 2019 Section 2(47).
   - RESOLVE-ONE provides direct, verified links and phone numbers to official portals.
   - The user retains 100% agency over what is submitted and when.

---

## 4. No Live Banking / Account Aggregator API Integration

### What was omitted:
Connecting to Indian Account Aggregators (Setu, Anumati, Finvu) or net-banking APIs to automatically scrape bank statements for refund credits.

### Why:
1. **Privacy Intrusion**: An Account Aggregator consent flow exposes months of personal banking transactions simply to verify a single ₹1,499 refund.
2. **Consent Fatigue & Revocation Risk**: Many consumers do not understand ongoing financial data sharing permissions or how to revoke them.
3. **The Refund Reality Check**: We solve the verification problem without invasive banking access by providing a side-by-side reconciliation interface:
   - Promised Amount vs Confirmed Amount.
   - Transparent three-state tracking: `Company Promised` -> `Evidence Found` -> `Customer Confirmed in Bank`.
   - Clear reminders prompting the user to cross-reference their actual banking app or passbook before closing a dispute.

---

## 5. No 44 "Engines", "Gates", or "Passports" in Consumer UI

### What was omitted:
The prototype's monolithic 22-item navigation menu, 44 esoteric "engines" (e.g., "Engine 17: Gate Guardian", "Engine 34: Quantum Passport"), and 150 internal capability checklists.

### Why:
1. **Cognitive Load on Stressed Users**: A user seeking a refund for spoiled groceries or defective electronics is overwhelmed by developer-centric jargon and sprawling checklists.
2. **Focused 5-Stream Architecture**: We consolidated 44 concepts into 5 obvious, human-language navigation destinations:
   - **Home / Overview**: What needs my attention right now?
   - **Cases**: What is the status of my disputes?
   - **Documents**: How do I export my proof pack?
   - **Purchases**: When do my return windows and warranties expire?
   - **Rights & Help**: What are my legal avenues under Indian law?
3. **Quarantine of Internal Capabilities**: The 150-feature capability register and architectural engine directory were preserved and moved to a quarantined, feature-flagged internal route (`/internal`), ensuring developer visibility without burdening consumers.

---

## Summary

By refusing to build fragile cloud databases, deceptive "AI" claims, credential-harvesting scrapers, and invasive bank integrations, RESOLVE-ONE delivers what consumers actually need: **speed, dignity, cryptographic evidence integrity, and calm control over their own disputes.**
